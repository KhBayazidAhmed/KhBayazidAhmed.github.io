# Before You Build
## A business website, app and software planning checklist
Created by BIXBD — [BIXBD](https://www.bixbd.com/)

### 1. Describe the goal
- What business problem should this project solve?
- Who will use it, and what should they be able to complete?
- What would make the first release useful?

### 2. Map one complete journey
- How does a customer discover you, ask questions, order or book, and get support?
- What does your team do at each step?
- Which information is currently repeated, missing or hard to find?

### 3. Choose the first release
- List the essential pages, screens and actions.
- Separate launch requirements from later ideas.
- Identify mobile, language and accessibility needs.

### 4. List the connections
- Does the project connect to payments, delivery, messaging or existing software?
- Who owns those accounts and can authorize access?
- What should happen when a connection is unavailable?

### 5. Prepare the content
- Gather the logo, business details, service descriptions and real images.
- Obtain permission for any client work, quotes or logos you plan to show.
- Confirm prices and claims before publishing.

### 6. Agree responsibilities
- Who approves the design, scope and content?
- Who provides feedback, and at which stages?
- Who will manage the system after launch?

### 7. Plan launch and support
- Agree the scope, quote and delivery date.
- Confirm testing, domain access, account ownership and backups.
- Arrange training and clarify the support agreement.
